    How to raise your California take-home pay

    • Contribute pre-tax. Every dollar into a 401(k) or HSA comes out of federal taxable income; the 2026 401(k) limit is $24,500.
    • Fix your W-4. Withholding is only as accurate as the form behind it — a mismatch shows up as a refund or a bill.
    • Know the FICA cap. Payroll tax on Social Security stops once year-to-date wages pass $184,500, so later paychecks jump.
    • Check the graduated state schedule. California brackets mean the top rate only applies to the last slice of income.

    California paycheck questions

    Does California have a state income tax?

    California uses a graduated income tax: 10 brackets running from 1% to 13.3%, with a $5,540 standard deduction for single filers ($11,080 for couples).

    What is the take-home pay on $50,000 in California?

    A single filer in California keeps about $40,503 a year — $3,375 a month, or $1,558 every two weeks — after $3,820 of federal tax, $3,825 of FICA and $1,202 of state income tax.

    What is the take-home pay on $100,000 in California?

    A single filer keeps about $72,657 a year ($6,055 a month) and a married couple filing jointly keeps about $81,005. The difference is mostly federal bracket progression plus the state's graduated brackets

    What else comes out of a California paycheck?

    Social Security at 6.2% up to the $184,500 wage base, Medicare at 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000, and any employer benefit premiums. Pre-tax retirement contributions such as a 401(k) reduce federal taxable income (2026 limit $24,500) but not FICA.
