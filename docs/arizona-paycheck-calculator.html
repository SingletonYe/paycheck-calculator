    How to raise your Arizona take-home pay

    • Contribute pre-tax. Every dollar into a 401(k) or HSA comes out of federal taxable income; the 2026 401(k) limit is $24,500.
    • Fix your W-4. Withholding is only as accurate as the form behind it — a mismatch shows up as a refund or a bill.
    • Know the FICA cap. Payroll tax on Social Security stops once year-to-date wages pass $184,500, so later paychecks jump.
    • Check the flat-rate state schedule. One flat rate applies from the first dollar, so the timing of bonuses barely changes the bill.

    Arizona paycheck questions

    Does Arizona have a state income tax?

    Arizona taxes wages at a flat rate of 2.5%, against a $8,350 standard deduction for single filers ($16,700 for couples).

    What is the take-home pay on $50,000 in Arizona?

    A single filer in Arizona keeps about $41,314 a year — $3,443 a month, or $1,589 every two weeks — after $3,820 of federal tax, $3,825 of FICA and $1,041 of state income tax.

    What is the take-home pay on $100,000 in Arizona?

    A single filer keeps about $76,889 a year ($6,407 a month) and a married couple filing jointly keeps about $82,628. The difference is mostly federal bracket progression plus withholding on your W-4

    What else comes out of a Arizona paycheck?

    Social Security at 6.2% up to the $184,500 wage base, Medicare at 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000, and any employer benefit premiums. Pre-tax retirement contributions such as a 401(k) reduce federal taxable income (2026 limit $24,500) but not FICA.
